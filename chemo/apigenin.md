Dietary flavone found widely in plants. One of the two key active compounds in [[justicia gendarussa]].

**Class:** flavone (aglycone)  
**Molecular formula:** C₁₅H₁₀O₅  
**CAS:** 520-36-5  
**Source (in this context):** *Justicia gendarussa* leaf

## mechanism

Inhibits NF-κB transcription factor and downstream COX-2 / iNOS expression. Blocks prostaglandin and nitric oxide synthesis in the inflammatory cascade. Functional analogue of NSAIDs (ibuprofen, diclofenac) without cyclooxygenase covalent binding and without associated gastrointestinal toxicity.

Additional activities: mild anxiolytic (GABA-A modulation), weak aromatase inhibitor, AMPK activator (longevity signaling).

## pharmacology

Onset: immediate at adequate tissue concentration  
Bioavailability: moderate; enhanced with fat-soluble cofactors  
Inhibition: 89% edema reduction in carrageenan rat model  
Safety: no significant toxicity at dietary/herbal doses in human studies

## occurrence

Widespread in plant kingdom: chamomile, parsley, celery, artichoke, peppermint. *Justicia gendarussa* is a concentrated source.
