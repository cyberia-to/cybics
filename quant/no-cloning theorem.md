no operation copies an unknown [[quantum]] state. linearity forbids it (Wootters–Zurek, Dieks 1982); optimal approximate cloning is capped at fidelity 5/6, and the bound is what stops entanglement from being used to signal faster than light

classical information has no such protection, and content addressing makes copying free: a [[particle]] is its own hash, so anyone can republish it perfectly

this is why [[cyber]] must *buy* what physics gets for nothing. a copied [[cyberlink]] produces the same focus shift as the original, so the [[Shapley value]] alone would split their credit evenly. the per-contribution surprise gate — [[Bayesian Truth Serum]] — restores the asymmetry: the copy enters the mint weightless. the honesty layer of the reward is an economic prosthesis for a theorem the classical world does not have
