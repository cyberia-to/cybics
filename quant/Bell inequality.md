loophole-free tests closed the detection and locality gaps simultaneously in 2015 (Hensen, Giustina, Shalm); the 2022 Nobel Prize followed. this is not interpretation — it is measurement

the consequence for any information-first model of the world is sharp: **a local classical stochastic process on a graph cannot reproduce physics.** positivity-preserving kernels have no amplitudes to cancel, and without cancellation there is no violation
