the simplest model of social learning (Morris DeGroot, 1974): each agent repeatedly replaces her opinion with a weighted average of her neighbours' opinions. the trust weights form a row-stochastic matrix, so by [[perron-frobenius-theorem]] the process converges to a consensus — and each agent's influence on that consensus is her entry in the stationary distribution

social influence is eigenvector centrality in disguise: the same mathematics that ranks [[particles]] by [[focus]] ranks people by trust
