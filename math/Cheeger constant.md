the tightest bottleneck of a graph: over all ways to cut the vertex set in two, the minimum ratio of edge boundary to the volume of the smaller side. small Cheeger constant means the graph has a thin waist; large means every region is well connected to the rest

it bounds the [[spectral gap]] from both sides (Cheeger inequality), so it controls how fast [[diffusion]] mixes and how fast [[focus]] converges on the [[cybergraph]]: a well-linked graph has no thin waist, a large gap, and geometric convergence of attention
